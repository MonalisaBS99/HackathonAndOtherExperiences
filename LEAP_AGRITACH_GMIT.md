🌾 GMIT LEAP Agritech Hackathon 2026

An Experience of Innovation, Teamwork and Learning

📌 About the Experience

Participated in the 24-Hour LEAP Agritech Hackathon 2026, organized by G. Madegowda Institute of Technology (GMIT), Mandya, in association with Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KITS).

The hackathon focused on developing innovative solutions for real-world agricultural challenges.

💡 Our Idea – Raitha Rassedi

We selected the Open Innovation track and explored the problem statement:

Paddy Procurement Transparency and Digital Transaction Record System.

Our idea, Raitha Rassedi, focused on helping paddy farmers maintain transparent digital records of quantities sold, weighing details, moisture assessments, deductions and payments.

We explored concepts such as:

- Kannada-first digital accessibility.
- Bhoomi – Kannada AI Voice Assistant.
- Offline-first functionality.
- Digital transaction receipts.
- Evidence documentation.
- Recheck and report mechanisms.

🎯 What We Learned

- Understanding real-world agricultural problems.
- Converting a problem statement into a proposed solution.
- Exploring AI-powered regional-language accessibility.
- Understanding the importance of transparency in agricultural procurement.
- Preparing technical presentations.
- Collaborating and working as a team under time constraints.

🤝 Team Experience

This experience was about more than the final selection. It taught us about preparation, teamwork, presenting ideas and understanding the expectations of a competitive hackathon.

Although we couldn't progress beyond the initial round, the experience gave us valuable insights into approaching future hackathons.
